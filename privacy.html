Privacy Policy.

Effective 1 November 2026. This policy describes how PINNED — Travel Journal handles information when you use the app.

1. Memories and local storage

PINNED is designed to store your saved places, notes, dates, collections and imported memory photos locally on your device. We do not operate a PINNED account service or a server that receives your memory entries.

2. Location and maps

PINNED uses Apple's mapping services to display maps and search for places. Location information you choose to use, such as a selected place or your current location if you grant permission, may be processed by Apple to provide map and search features. Apple's own privacy terms apply to those services. You can control location permissions in iOS Settings.

3. Photos and camera

When you choose photos or take a picture, PINNED accesses only the photos or camera functions needed for that action, subject to iOS permissions. Photos added to memories are stored on your device. You can change permissions in iOS Settings.

4. Purchases

PINNED Pro subscriptions and lifetime purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. Apple handles payment details. The app can access purchase and entitlement status to unlock paid features; we do not receive your full payment card details.

5. Analytics and advertising

PINNED does not include third-party advertising or a developer-operated analytics tracking service. Apple may process information under its own policies when providing App Store, purchase and mapping services.

6. Sharing and exports

If you export a memory image or share content, you decide where to send it. The destination app or service handles shared content according to its own privacy policy.

7. Data retention and deletion

Your local memories remain on your device until you delete them or remove the app. Deleting the app may remove locally stored memories. Apple may retain purchase records according to its policies.

8. Children's privacy

PINNED does not intentionally collect personal information through a developer-operated account or tracking system. Users should avoid including sensitive personal information in content they choose to share.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. Any revised policy will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email support@kook.love.

This policy reflects the described local-first app design. Review it against the final shipped app, any SDKs and enabled cloud services before publishing.
