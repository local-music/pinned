Frequently asked questions

How do I add a memory?

Open PINNED, choose Add Place, select a location, then add your photos, notes and visit date.

How many places can I save for free?

The free version supports up to three saved places. PINNED Pro unlocks additional places and premium features.

How do I restore my PINNED Pro purchase?

Open the app's purchase or settings screen and select Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account used for the original purchase. If you still need help, email us.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions, then select PINNED. Apple manages subscription billing and cancellation.

What happens if I delete the app?

Memories stored only on your device may be lost when the app is deleted. Use any available backup or export features before removing the app. Contact us if you need help.
